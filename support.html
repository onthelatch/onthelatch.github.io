Support

House Manager for iPhone, iPad, iPod touch and Android

Need a hand with House Manager? Email us at dev@onthelat.ch. We usually reply within one working day, and faster on show days if you put "URGENT" in the subject line.

When you email us, it helps to include:

  • Your venue's name and your Spektrix® Client ID
  • The device you're using (for example, iPhone 13 on iOS 18, or Samsung Galaxy A54)
  • What you were doing, what happened, and roughly when, so we can match it to your Spektrix® records
  • The exact wording of any message the app showed, or a screenshot

Please don't send us your Spektrix® password or your patrons' details. We never need them.

What you need

  • A House Manager licence: your licence key is in the email you received after purchase.
  • A Spektrix® account with Scanning User permissions. Scan reports, the attendee list and access notes also need a little setup in Spektrix®. See the Setup Guide.
  • A supported device:
    • iPhone, iPad or iPod touch running iOS or iPadOS 15 or later
    • Android phones and tablets with a camera (over 98% of Android devices)

Door Sales is available in the Android app only. The iPhone and iPad app has Scan Mode, Offline Mode, the Scan Report, the Attendee List and Access Notes.

Getting started

1. Install the app from the App Store (iPhone, iPad and iPod touch) or Google Play (Android).

2. Activate the device. Enter your licence key and your Spektrix® Client ID. Your Client ID is the part of your Spektrix® web address after system.spektrix.com/.

3. Log in to Spektrix® with the username and password of your scanning account.

4. Allow camera access when the app asks, so it can read ticket barcodes.

Troubleshooting

The camera shows a black screen or won't scan

The app needs permission to use the camera.

  • iPhone and iPad: go to Settings › House Manager and turn on Camera.
  • Android: go to Settings › Apps › House Manager › Permissions and allow Camera.

Hold the ticket so the whole barcode is inside the camera view. If the ticket is on a patron's phone, ask them to turn their screen brightness up.

"Invalid licence key."

Check the key against your licence email, character for character, including any hyphens.

"Client ID does not match this licence key."

Each licence is tied to one Spektrix® Client ID. Check that you've entered the same Client ID you gave at checkout. If your venue's Client ID has changed, email us.

"Device no longer active. Activate it again."

Another device has taken this device's place on your licence. Activate this device again if you need it, and the device that has gone unused the longest will make way.

"Cannot activate. Licence is full and unable to free a slot."

Your licence's devices are all still in use, so none could make way for this one. Log out on a device you don't need, or email us to add licences.

The Scan Report, Attendee List or Access Notes are empty

These features read a report from your Spektrix® system. Check that:

  • your account has the Collect Report role;
  • the report and report schedule are set up and named exactly as in the Setup Guide;
  • your account's email address is on the report schedule's delivery list.

Scans made offline haven't appeared in Spektrix®

Offline scans are kept on the device and sent to Spektrix® when the app reconnects. Keep the app open on a working connection until it shows that the sync is complete. Don't log out or delete the app while scans are waiting, or they'll be lost.

Licences and devices

  • One licence covers one device at a time. Log out on one device and activate another, and you're still using a single licence. iPhones, iPads and Android devices can share the same licence key.
  • Adding or removing licences: email us at dev@onthelat.ch.
  • Subscription and payment details are handled by our payment provider, Paddle. Use the Paddle customer portal, or the links in your payment confirmation emails.

Your data

Your Spektrix® login is kept on your device and sent only to Spektrix®. Patron names, tickets and access notes pass only between your device and Spektrix®. The only thing the app sends us is what we need to check your licence: your licence key, your Client ID and an identifier for the device. Read the full Privacy Policy.

To see the device records we hold for your licence, or to have one removed, email dev@onthelat.ch from the address on your licence.
